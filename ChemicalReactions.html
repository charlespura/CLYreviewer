Combination (Synthesis) Decomposition Single Replacement Double Replacement Combustion

Combination

A + B → AB

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

Two or more substances combine to form one product

Decomposition

AB → A + B

2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂

One compound breaks down into simpler substances

Single Replacement

A + BC → AC + B

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

One element replaces another in a compound

Double Replacement

AB + CD → AD + CB

AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Two compounds exchange ions to form new compounds

Combustion

Fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O

Substance reacts with oxygen, releasing heat and light

Photosynthesis

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Plants convert sunlight, CO₂, and water into glucose and oxygen

Grilling: New compounds form (aroma)
Fermentation: CO₂ bubbles produced
Candle Burning: Wax vapor + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O
Digestion: Large molecules → smaller nutrients
Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O → Glucose + O₂
Haber Process: N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (fertilizer)
Combustion Engines: Fuel + O₂ → Energy + emissions
Acid Rain: Acids react with carbonates → weathering
