Audiovisual & Music Innovation

  • Phonograph (1877) – first recorded music in homes
  • Electric Guitar (1940s) – birth of Rock 'n' Roll
  • Synthesizer – infinite orchestral sounds electronically
  • Multitrack Recording – piece-by-piece studio magic
  • Pitch Manipulation (AutoTune) & Vocoder – modify vocal intonation
  • PC & Internet – bedroom talents go global without major studio backing

Digital Interactive Media

Karaoke vs Videoke: Karaoke (Japan 1971) · Videoke (PH, Roberto del Rosario, 1975) – interactive scoring, video bg, pitch control.

Game Music: dopamine boost, modular cues, emotional variation. Treat the game canvas like an interactive film.

Philippine context: rising frontier, but faces scarcity of highly trained software developers and resistance to microtransaction monetization.
